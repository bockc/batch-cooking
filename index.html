Storage

Keep it fresh through Friday.

Cool completely before sealing. Most plates keep 4 days in the fridge; freeze anything past day 4 the same night you cook.

  • Proteins — store sauces separate when possible so textures stay sharp on reheat.
  • Grains & legumes — portion with a splash of stock or water so they don’t dry out.
  • Greens — roast sturdy veg (broccoli, peppers); keep fresh salad for day-of only.
  • Reheat — oven or skillet beats microwave for chicken and fish; add a squeeze of citrus after.
